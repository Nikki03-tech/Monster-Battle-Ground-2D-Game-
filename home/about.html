ABOUT GAME

Monster Battle Ground is an intense 2D action game where players face relentless waves of monstrous enemies in a fast-paced battle arena. Armed with various weapons, players must strategically dodge attacks, defeat enemies, and survive increase in difficulty to play. Each monster has unique AI behavior, ranging from aggressive melee fighters to deadly ranged attackers with special abilities. As the game progresses, powerful bosses emerge, requiring skill and strategy to defeat. The game features immersive sound effects, animations, and a fully functional menu system, including a start menu, pause options, and settings for a complete and engaging experience.
